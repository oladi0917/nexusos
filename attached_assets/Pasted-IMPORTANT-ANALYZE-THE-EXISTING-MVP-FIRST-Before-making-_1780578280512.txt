CORE VISION

Nexus is not a bank.

Nexus is a financial control platform that allows users to:

Connect multiple bank accounts
Connect debit cards
Connect multiple phone numbers
View all balances in one place
Track transactions across platforms
Send money from multiple sources simultaneously
Manage a Nexus Wallet
Authorize transfers with one Nexus PIN
Tag incoming credit alerts
Control their financial life from one interface

Goal:

One place to see, manage, and move all your money.
